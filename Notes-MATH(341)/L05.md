# Rectangular Systems - Echelon Form

> [!ex] Example: Homogenous System
> $$ \begin{bmatrix} x & x & x \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ x \\ x \end{bmatrix} = b \\ $$
>
> $$
> 	\begin{bmatrix}
> 		\mathbf{1} & 2 & 2           & 3  & | & 0 \\
> 		0          & 0 & \mathbf{-3} & -3 & | & 0
> \end{bmatrix}
> $$
>
> Note $\mathbf{1,-3}$ are pivots.
>
> $$
> 	\begin{align}
> 		\text{Parametric Vector Form} \\
> 		x_4 = \text{Free}             \\
> 		x_3 = -x_4                    \\
> 		x_2 = \text{Free}             \\
> 		x_1 = -2x_2 - 2x_3 - 3x_4
> \end{align}
> 	\implies Nul(A) =
> 	\vec{x_2} \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} +
> 	\vec{x_4} \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
> $$

> [!def] General Expressesion of Homogenous Systems
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> & \text{In general let } A \in R^{m \times n}, \text{ rank}(A)=r \text{ then} \\
> & r ... \text{pivots and } n-r \text{ free variables }                        \\
> & \implies \text{ the solution can be expressed as follows: }                 \\
> & x = x_{f_1}h_1 + x_{f_2}h_2 + ..._ + x_{f_{n-r}}h_{n-r}
> \end{align}
> $$

> [!ex] Example: Non-Homogenous Systems
>
> $$
> 	\begin{bmatrix}
> 		1 & 2 & 0 & 1 & | & 2 \\
> 		0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & | & 1
> \end{bmatrix} \implies
> 	\text{ Parametric Vector Form}
> 	\begin{array}{cc}
> & x_4 = f              \\
> & x_2 = 1-x_4          \\
> & x_3 = f              \\
> & x_1 = 2 - 2x_3 - x_4
> \end{array} \implies
> 	\begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} +
> 	\vec{x_3} \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} +
> 	\vec{x_4} \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 0 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}
> $$
>
> $$ \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} $$
> $$ \text{Represents the particular solution.} $$

# Four Fundamental Subspaces
$$ \text{Consider $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$} $$

# Row/Col Space
> [!def] Row/Col Space
> The Row/Col Space can be defined as:
>
> $$
> 	\begin{align}
> 		\text{Col Space := } & R(A) = \{ Ax | x \in \mathbb{R}^n \} \subseteq R^m     \\
> 		\text{Row Space := } & R(A^T) = \{ A^Ty | y \in \mathbb{R}^m \} \subseteq R^n
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> Thus we can say the following:
>
> $$
> 	\begin{align}
> & b \in R(A) \equiv b \in Col(A)    \iff \exists ~x \ni b=Ax   \\
> & a \in R(A^T) \equiv  b \in Row(A) \iff \exists ~y \ni a=A^Ty
> \end{align}
> $$

# Null Space
> [!def] Null Space
>
> $$
>     Nul(A) = \{ x | Ax = \vec{0} \} \subseteq R^n
> $$

# Left-Hand Nullspace
> [!def] Left-Hand Nullspace
>
> $$
>     Nul(A^T) = \{ y | A^Ty = \vec{0} \} \subseteq R^m
> $$

# What are the spanning sets for the Fundamental subspaces?
> [!cor] What are the spanning sets for the Fundamental subspaces?
>
> $$
> 	\begin{align}
> & Col(A) \equiv R(A)   &  & \implies  \text{Pivot Cols of A}                  \\
> & Nul(A)               &  & \implies \text{ Particular Solutions of $Ax=0$}   \\
> & Row(A) \equiv R(A^T) &  & \implies  \text{Pivot Rows of EF/REF}             \\
> & N(A^T)               &  & \implies \text{ Particular Solutions of $A^Ty=0$}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> > [!tip] Regarding $N(A^T)$
> > Consider $P$ from [LU Factorization](1770301034-TEDP.md)
> > $$ \text{Consider: } PA=EF \implies rank(A) = r \implies P = \begin{bmatrix} P_1 & \text{r rows} \\ P_2 & \text{m-r rows} \end{bmatrix} \implies N(A^T) = R(P_2^T) $$

# Orthogonal Compliment
> [!def] Orthogonal Compliment
> $$ \text{Let } V \text{ be a vector space.} $$
> $$ \text{Let } M \subseteq V \text{ We can define an orhogonal compliment: } M^{\perp} \text{ of } M $$
>
> $$
> \begin{align}
> M^{\perp} := \{ x \in V ~:~ \forall ~m \in M, ~<m|v> ~=0 \}
> \end{align}
> $$
>
> ## Direct Sum
> > [!cor] Direct Sun
> > Note $\oplus$ here is called a _direct sum_ (below):
> > $$ M \subseteq V \implies V = M \oplus M^{\perp} $$
> > This states the following:
> > $$ \forall ~v \in V, ~v = ~!m + ~!m^{\perp} $$
> > Where all $v$ can be expressed as a _unique!_ linear combination of the components in $M \oplus M^{\perp}$

# Orthogonality of Subspaces
> [!def] Orthogonality of Subspaces
> Consider:
> $$ \text{Inner Product: } <x|y> = x^{\perp}y $$
> $$ \text{Is there any orthogonality between any fundamental subspaces?} $$
> Consider:
> $$ x \in Row(A) \implies <x|Ay> = 0 $$ 
> 
> Observe:
> $$
> \forall ~y, ~ <x|Ay> = <Ay | x > = (Ay)^Tx = y^TA^Tx = <y|A^Tx> = 0 \\
> $$
> 
> $$ \therefore ~R(A)^{\perp} = N(A^{T}) \iff N(A) = (R(A^T))^{\perp} $$
