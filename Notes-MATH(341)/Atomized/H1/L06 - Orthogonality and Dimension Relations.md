# Orthogonality and Dimension Relations

*Original Note: [[L06]]*

- Orthogonality relations:
  - $R(A)^\perp = N(A^T)$ in $\mathbb{R}^m$,
  - $R(A^T)^\perp = N(A)$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$.

- Dimension relations (rank–nullity):
  - $\dim R(A) = r$, $\dim N(A^T) = m-r$,
  - $\dim R(A^T) = r$, $\dim N(A) = n-r$.

- Direct sums:
  $$\mathbb{R}^n = N(A) \oplus R(A^T), \qquad \mathbb{R}^m = R(A) \oplus N(A^T).$$
