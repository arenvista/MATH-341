# Null Space and Left Null Space

*Original Note: [[L05 - Four Fundamental Subspaces]]*

> [!def] Null spaces
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \mathrm{Nul}(A) &= \{x\in\mathbb{R}^n : Ax=\mathbf{0}\},\\
> \mathrm{Nul}(A^\top) &=
> \{y\in\mathbb{R}^m : A^\top y=\mathbf{0}\}\quad\text{(left null space)}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
