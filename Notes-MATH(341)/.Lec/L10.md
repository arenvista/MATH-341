Recall:

$$
	\begin{gather}
		\left\| Ax-b \right\| = \dots = \left\| Rx-Q^Tb \right\| \\
		A = QR \\
		A^TAx = A^Tb \\
		(QR)^T QRx = (QR)^Tb \\
		R^TQ^TQRx = R^TQ^Tb \\
	\end{gather}
$$

$$
	\begin{bmatrix}
		r_1 \\ r_2 \\ \vdots \\ r_n \\ r_{n+1} \\ \vdots \\ r_m
	\end{bmatrix} =
	\begin{bmatrix}
		r_{1,1} & \dots  & r_{1,n} \\
		\vdots  & \ddots & \vdots  \\
		.       &        & r_{m,n}
	\end{bmatrix}
$$

Note $R$ is an upper tirabgular matrix and $r_{i,i} \neq 0 \implies R$ is upper triangular.

If $R$ is _non singular_ then we can multiply by $R^{-T}$

$$
	\begin{gather}
		R^TQ^TQRx = R^TQ^Tb \\
		(R^{-T})R^TQ^TQRx = (R^{-T})R^TQ^Tb \\
		IRx = Q^Tb \\
		Rx = Q^Tb \\
	\end{gather}
$$

# Floating Point Arithmatic

$$
	\text{Floating Point Arithmatic: } f := d_1 d_2 d_3 \dots d_t \times \beta^e
$$

Note Components:

- Mantissa: $d_1 d_2 d_3 \dots d_t$
- Base: $\beta$
- Exponent: $e$

In general, $fl(x) :=$ defines the floating point representation of $x$

$$
	\begin{gather}
		\text{Recall: } \\
		fl(x+y) \neq fl(x) + fl(y) \\
		fl(xy) \neq fl(x) \cdot fl(y)
	\end{gather}
$$

Example:

$$
	\text{Add: }
	\begin{array}{ccc}
		-10^{-4}x + y & = & 1 \\
		x + y         & = & 2
	\end{array}
$$

Paper Calculation:

$$
	\begin{gather}
		\left[
			\begin{array}{cc|c}
				-10^{-4} & 1 & 1 \\
				1        & 1 & 2
			\end{array}
			\right] \xrightarrow{R_2 = R_1 \times 1E4}
		\left[
			\begin{array}{cc|c}
				-10^{-4} & 1    & 1    \\
				0        & 1001 & 1002
			\end{array}
			\right] \\
		\implies x = -10^{-4}(1-y)
	\end{gather}
$$

Computer Calculation:

$$
	\begin{gather}
		\left[
			\begin{array}{cc|c}
				-10^{-4} & 1 & 1 \\
				1        & 1 & 2
			\end{array}
			\right] \xrightarrow{R_2 = R_1 \times 1E4}
		\left[
			\begin{array}{cc|c}
				-10^{-4} & 1   & 1   \\
				0        & 1E4 & 2E4
			\end{array}
			\right] \xrightarrow{\tiny{\text{Floating Pt. Truncation}}}
		\left[
			\begin{array}{cc|c}
				-10^{-4} & 1   & 1   \\
				0        & 1E4 & 2E4
			\end{array}
			\right] \\
		\implies y=1, ~x=0 \implies  (x,y) = (0,1) \\
		\text{How can we fix this?  Adjust the pivots!}
	\end{gather}
$$

# Vector Norm

$$ \text{A norm is a function } \|\cdot\| : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R} \text{ such that, for all } x,y \in \mathbb{R}^n \text{ and all scalars } \alpha: \\ $$

$$
	\begin{array}{ccc}
		 & \text{1. (Positive definiteness)} & \quad \|x\| \ge 0,\ \ \|x\| = 0 \iff x = 0 \\
		 & \text{2. (Absolute homogeneity)}  & \quad \|\alpha x\| = |\alpha|\,\|x\|       \\
		 & \text{3. (Triangle inequality)}   & \quad \|x+y\| \le \|x\| + \|y\|
	\end{array}
$$

## Examples

$$
	\begin{align}
		\|x\|_1      & = \sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|                                              &  & \text{(}\ell_1\text{)}      \\
		\|x\|_2      & = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^2\right)^{1/2} = (x^\top x)^{1/2}      &  & \text{(}\ell_2\text{)}      \\
		\|x\|_\infty & = \max_{1 \le i \le n} |x_i|                                        &  & \text{(}\ell_\infty\text{)} \\
		\|x\|_p      & = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} |x_i|^p\right)^{1/p}, \quad 1 \le p < \infty &  & \text{(}\ell_p\text{)}
	\end{align}
$$

$$
	\text{(Submultiplicativity for induced matrix norms)} \quad \|AB\| \le \|A\|\,\|B\|
$$

# Fuburis Norm (Non-Induced: Coressponds w/ ii.)
$$
\begin{gather}
\left\| A \right\|_F = (\sum_{i}^{}\sum_{j}^{} \left| x_{ij} \right|^2  )^{\frac{1}{2}} \\
tr(A) := \sum_i |x_{i,i}| \\
\sqrt{tr(A^TA)} = \sqrt{tr(AA^T)}
\end{gather}
$$ 

$$
\begin{gather}
\left\| A \right\|_i = max\left( \sum_{i=1}^{m} \left| a_{ij} \right|\right) ~ \equiv max(\left\| a_j \right\|_i), ~~~~1 \leq j \leq n \\
\left\| A \right\|_j = max\left( \sum_{j=1}^{m} \left| a_{ij} \right|\right) ~ \equiv max(\left\| a_i \right\|_j), ~~~~1 \leq i \leq m \\
\end{gather}
$$ 

# Condition Number of a Matrix
$$
k(a) = \left\| A \right\| \cdot  \left\| A^{-1} \right\|
$$ 
Observations:
$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
a. k(I) = 1 \\
b. k(A) > 1 \\
\end{array}
$$ 

$$
\begin{gather}
\left\| I \right\| =  \left\| A A^{-1} \right\| = \left\| A \right\| \left\| A^{-1} \right\| \\
\text{Triangle Inequality: }  \left\| AA^{-1} \right\| \leq  \left\| A \right\| \left\| A^{-1} \right\|
\end{gather}
$$ 

Remark: Compleness have hold that look with singular and nonsigular, but with rows and columns "almost" linearly dependent.
